Correction to the retained eight-study breadth audit
==================================================
The original CSV/Markdown are preserved as historical coding, not silently
overwritten. The original LSGen row described an arXiv version and stated that
no one-iteration equal-generation-budget control was located. Rechecking the
published AAAI 2026 paper shows LSGENbase vs LSGENiter3 (Table 2), one-iteration
ablations (Table 3), and an iteration-count curve from 0 to 3 (Figure 3b).
It is therefore incorrect to suggest LSGen has no single-stage/iteration
comparison. Adaptive retrieval iterations and a fixed independent-draw union
are different interventions; this distinction does not invalidate LSGen's
own ablation. The manuscript no longer uses the three-of-five missing-control
count. The five multi-opportunity entries and eight-study scope are unchanged.

Source: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40300/44261
Publication: AAAI 40(36), 30467-30475 (2026), DOI 10.1609/aaai.v40i36.40300.
LSGen also builds retrieval pairs from each user's chronological incorrect and
later-correct submissions (Solution Retrieval Database Construction, p.30469).
Consequently the manuscript does not claim first use of submission trajectories.

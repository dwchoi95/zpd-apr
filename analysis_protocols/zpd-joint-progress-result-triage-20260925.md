# Joint4131 result triage — outcome-blind checklist

2026-09-25. Prepared while the experiment is running, **without reading its
results, models, predictions or label bodies**. No success is assumed. This
checklist neither changes the frozen protocol nor authorizes another fit,
execution, outcome access, exclusion, threshold search or control promotion.

Read the complete frozen prospective protocol, the completed negative
progress32 diagnosis and its independent review. The previous H205 versus
structural C206 result remains negative. Its class/radius observations motivate

## 1. Establish that there is a result to interpret

- Authenticate the supplied outer/run/independent-audit pins and all consumed
  bindings. Require all320 recipients,23 problems,2544 candidates,35 models,
  five scalers,14 methods and five unchanged folds; exact gate agreement;
  no failed/rejected completion, omitted cell or substituted model.
- An operational/numerical failure is an incomplete experiment, **not** a
  failed scientific gate or a new RR estimate. Preserve partial artifacts;
  do not score a favorable completed subset or splice a resumed run.
- For a verified completion, preserve its original report and every negative
  gate. New diagnostics go to separately sealed outputs and are labeled
  post-completion adaptive-development analyses.

## 2. Read all four gates before any explanatory case study

| Frozen gate | Question; required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Repair | Does H meet every original baseline, pooled/equal-problem, both regression-bound, current-status and fold condition? |
| History mechanism | Does H meet every pooled Jm/HS/U/L comparison and four-of-five-fold condition? Do not invent an equal-problem test or drop a difficult control. |
| CF | Is authentic residual history useful beyond the same current-feedback access, under every full/subgroup/fold condition? |
| C_joint | Does H meet the additional matched joint-current-only comparison? This is an AND, not a rescue gate. |

Copy each Boolean and its exact failed subconditions, not only aggregate RR.
Success is their conjunction. Repair improvement without history/CF support
does not establish the proposed history mechanism. Mechanism support without
the repair gate does not establish a useful repair improvement. A winning
control/shared-H diagnostic cannot become the primary. Even all four passing
would be adaptive fixed-bank evidence, not fresh generalization or learner/ZPD
causal effectiveness.

## 3. Explain changes with complete paired accounting

- For H against structural_C, lexical_C, first_attempt, each fitted control and
  each shared-H substitution, record changed choices, AC gained/lost, regression
  prevented/introduced, to/from-no-op versus edit-to-edit, and not-jointly-known.
  A non-AC selection is not automatically a regression. Never recode unknown
  as failure, no regression or success.
- Report pooled and equal-problem values with both identification bounds, all
  five folds and original known-AC/nonAC/unknown groups. Bounds are not sampling
  confidence intervals. Equal aggregate totals do not mean identical choices.
- Partition exact H/control feature equality versus inequality without dropping
  either partition. Separately fitted coefficients can change choices even on
  equal-feature banks. Use shared-H HS/Jm/CF, which fixes **all4131** coefficients,
  to measure direct computational input sensitivity. It is not a learner causal
  effect. Keep exact feature equality distinct from predeclared geometric tau.

## 4. Diagnose misses in an order that avoids conflating causes

1. **Candidate availability:** on all banks, count known-AC oracle opportunities,
   possible-only opportunities involving unknowns, and banks without either.
   Partition H's misses accordingly. An oracle is diagnostic only; never route,
   choose candidates or select a new cohort using evaluation labels.
2. **Representation support:** within known-AC opportunities, inspect exact
   complete4131 candidate-feature equivalence and SHA ties. Do not reuse the
   old fixed-base H32 class-champion ceiling as a joint-model theorem: current
   coefficients now change. Distinguishable alternatives need not be jointly
   realizable by one shared linear model.
3. **Actual margins and direction:** for each known-AC miss, use the existing
   frozen H scores to identify its highest-scoring known-AC alternative solely
   for analysis. Decompose selected-minus-alternative score into current4099,
   common current-feedback16 and authentic residual16 contributions. Compare
   with the sealed shared-H CF choice. Distinguish zero/weak residual leverage,
   wrong-direction changes and unresolved current/interaction ranking error.
   Do not apply the previous fixed-base radius certificate to a reweighted
   joint score, or infer that increasing a coefficient norm would help.
4. **Observed support versus usefulness:** retain zero/singleton opportunity,
   constant feedback, unavailable/unknown, and authentic WA-to-WA off-diagonal
   strata. Readiness PASS proves specified nondegeneracy, not correct repair
   direction, adequate prevalence or transferable history information. Diagnose
   all strata descriptively; none is a replacement estimand or next-fit filter.

These causes can overlap. If a disjoint summary is needed, use the listed
availability-then-equivalence ordering and keep overlapping margin/support
statistics separately; do not add overlapping counts as independent failures.

## 5. Keep the old architecture comparison non-causal

Read only the pre-pinned completed progress32 H reference after the new full
barrier. Report whole-cohort paired scores/choices, unknowns, folds and subgroups.
Joint H beating old H alone cannot establish fixed-base suppression as the
cause: current weights and regularized parameter space also changed. Compare
C_joint and CF and direct shared-H sensitivity before attributing any change

## 6. Choose at most one evidence-supported next branch

- **Known-AC alternatives exist, but history has little/wrong direct sensitivity
  or fails matched controls:** do not amplify C/residuals or promote CF. First
  formulate one source-only candidate-local history representation hypothesis
  that addresses the measured equivalence/direction failure, with the same
  current-feedback-access control and a falsifying control. Require a new
  prospective feasibility/design decision; absence of identifiable history
  signal is a legitimate stopping conclusion for this representation.
- **Candidate absence, rather than ranking among known repairs, accounts for
  the relevant limitation:** a separately specified candidate-generation study
  may be the next hypothesis. Freeze its sampling and budgets before generating
  anything; do not regenerate only observed misses or pool changed banks into
  this experiment. No generation is authorized by this checklist.

If all gates pass, the next scientific question is independent generalization
under a separately frozen, authorized evaluation—not another development-bank
tuning cycle or an immediate educational claim. If evidence does not separate
the competing causes, report that uncertainty and do not choose a mechanism
by its most favorable subgroup. Any subsequent experiment needs its own fixed
protocol, source/input/runtime pins and authority. The present gates and all
320 records remain immutable in every branch.
